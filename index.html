Hogwarts history

Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry was founded over a thousand years ago in Scotland by four powerful witches and wizards: Godric Gryffindor, Helga Hufflepuff, Rowena Ravenclaw, and Salazar Slytherin.

They created Hogwarts to teach young witches and wizards magic and divided students into four houses based on personality and abilities. Over time, Hogwarts became one of the most respected wizarding schools and played an important role in the story of Harry Potter, especially during the fight against dark forces.
